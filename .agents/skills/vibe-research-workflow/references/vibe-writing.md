# Vibe Writing: evidence-grounded drafting and revision

Apply the shared [research autonomy and validation policy](../../shared-references/research-autonomy.md).
AI can draft and revise scientific arguments from actual research materials.
The user retains academic responsibility and decides material changes of
direction or final commitments; ordinary wording edits do not need individual
approval.

## Establish the task

Read the current research question, analyzed findings, verified literature and
existing draft. Distinguish new drafting, structural revision and language-only
polish. A candidate explanation or proposed future test must not become a completed
finding. Reuse the [results-to-writing handoff](../../experiment-bridge/references/experiment-records.md#results-to-writing-handoff).

## Use the existing writing skills

- `good-story` organizes the supported argument when its structure needs work.
- `paper-writer` / `intro-drafter` compose new prose from the available evidence.
- `paper-polish` revises language while preserving facts, scope and justified
  claim strength.
- `pre-submission-reviewer` checks the requested manuscript scope when review
  is part of the task.

Use the shared [academic prose guidance](../../paper-polish/references/ai-tone-guardrails.md)
for complex-sentence inspection, locating problems before rewriting, direct
expression and the author's dash restriction. Apply contextual checks rather than
a second vocabulary blacklist or a mandatory author-style profile.

## Verify before delivery

The agent compares substantive claims with source artifacts, preserves numerical
and citation accuracy, and checks edits against the original meaning. Distinguish
language corrections from changes to scientific claims; substantive changes follow
[claim preservation](../../shared-references/claim-preservation.md) and existing
authorization. Missing context is not a reason to silently weaken a conclusion.

Keep the author's requested editing scope. A sentence-level correction does not
reopen topic selection or require a new experiment. A material unresolved evidence
gap is reported with its location and effect; plausible prose cannot fill it.

## Human review and final use

Provide usable text and concise notes on consequential changes or unresolved
scientific questions. The user reviews central claims and final commitments using
that evidence, without a mandatory rewrite or individual signoff for every
AI-assisted sentence. Apply verified venue requirements where relevant.

AI-use disclosure remains a separate draft under `ai-use-drafter`; wording
assistance does not authorize inserting it into the manuscript or a submission
form. Stop after the requested writing or review task.
