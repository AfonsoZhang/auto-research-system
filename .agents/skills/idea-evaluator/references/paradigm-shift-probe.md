# Optional paradigm-shift exploration

Use the [shared topic-selection policy](../../shared-references/topic-selection-policy.md).
This probe explores strategic upside; it is not an acceptance or topic-selection
gate. Focused incremental work, combinations, audits and negative findings remain
eligible regardless of the answers. Do not assign publication odds from a probe score.

When useful or requested, consider:

1. **First principles:** does the idea challenge a specific assumption, and what
   evidence would distinguish the old and new accounts?
2. **Neglected problem:** what meaningful question remains unanswered, and why
   might it now be tractable?
3. **Changed conditions:** has a verified change in tools, data or deployment
   opened a new setting or removed a constraint?
4. **Scientific/practical consequence:** whose understanding or design choices
   would change if the proposed result held, including a specialized community?

State supported possibilities and uncertainty in words. A surprising story does
not establish a paradigm shift, and a method that could have been tried years
ago can still yield a new finding. Do not require a community-wide breakthrough,
a first top-venue paper before ambitious work, or fixed portfolio percentages.

For deeper exploration, select the relevant adjacent references:
`paradigm-first-principles.md`, `paradigm-elephant.md`,
`paradigm-technology-cycle.md`, `paradigm-hamming.md` and
`paradigm-examples.md`. These are idea-generation lenses, not alternative veto
rules. Preserve the user's chosen scope; do not automatically expand a focused
topic into a broader program. Any draft conclusion remains hypothetical until tested.
