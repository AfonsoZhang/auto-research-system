# Research work and human responsibility

Apply the shared [research autonomy and validation policy](../../shared-references/research-autonomy.md).
It is the local source for delegation, scientific acceptance and human decision
points, including when older workflow advice conflicts with it.

## AI participation

AI may generate candidate ideas and hypotheses, compare explanations, design
experiments, implement, analyze, draw figures and draft prose within the user's
objective and authorized task. Scientific work is not restricted to mechanical
assistance. A generated hypothesis, planned experiment or proposed interpretation
is labeled as such until the appropriate evidence is available.

## Human decisions

The user controls the goal, budget, material direction choices and academic
responsibility. The agent investigates options and handles routine work and
verification. Ask about meaningful tradeoffs or missing authority, not every
technical choice. Existing delegated card decisions remain usable in the mode
and scope the user authorized.

## Verification

Execution completion and scientific support are separate conclusions. Inspect
the protocol, actual runs, original evidence, analysis and resulting claims.
Use existing experimental and writing checks, with independent QA for pivotal
claims where available. Do not declare that the user personally reviewed every
line or sentence; provide inspectable results for their scientific judgment.

For citations, apply the [publication policy](../../shared-references/publication-policy.md):
verify the work and version and check the source for the claimed content.
References need not originate in the user's own search. Missing or unreadable
evidence stays unverified rather than being invented or attributed to the user.

## Accurate records and disclosure

Preserve raw data, failed runs, exclusions, protocol amendments and AI assistance.
Do not fabricate results, procedures, citations, authorship or disclosure history.
Keep planned, simulated and measured outputs distinguishable.

Use verified requirements for the actual venue/institution when relevant to the
task. Do not infer a universal ban on AI drafting or require human rewriting
merely because AI supplied the first draft. AI-use declarations remain independent
drafts for the user to revise under `ai-use-drafter`.

## Examples

- The agent proposes hypotheses and designs a discriminating pilot within an
  agreed question and budget, then reports the protocol and actual results.
- The agent drafts Related Work from verified sources and checks each material
  attribution; the user reviews the resulting scientific positioning.
- A run exits successfully but uses the wrong split. Execution is complete, but
  that comparison is not accepted as evidence for the intended claim.
- A valid, sufficiently informative pilot meets the predeclared scientific
  stopping rule. The agent preserves the negative evidence and stops the dependent
  campaign under the [pilot outcome rules](../../experiment-plan/SKILL.md#gate-further-investment-with-a-minimum-decisive-pilot).
- A pilot remains inconclusive. Main runs stay pending while an authorized, bounded
  discriminating follow-up is considered. A budget pause preserves that unresolved
  finding rather than converting it into refutation.
- A needed resource exceeds the authorized budget. The agent presents concrete
  options while completing independent preparation.
